### Migration defect found and fixed

The generated migrations **only worked against an empty database**. `db:migrate`
crashed three times on the dev DB (30 companies / 33 MOUs / 220 payroll rows).
The integration suite never saw it because the test DB is always built from
scratch. Hand-written into the generated files:

- `0006`: add `company.sector` and `mou.generated_code` nullable → backfill →
  `SET NOT NULL`. Sector → `OTHER`. MOU codes rebuilt in the new
  `MOU-<SCHEME>-<YYYY>-<NNNN>` form with
  `row_number() OVER (PARTITION BY scheme, EXTRACT(YEAR FROM mou_start_date))`
  (can't copy `company.generated_code`: 3 companies hold 2 MOUs, and 0007 drops
  that column anyway). 15 of the 22 retired payroll keys are folded into
  `OTHER` with `initcap(replace(item_key,'_',' '))` as the label, not deleted.
- `0007`: `DELETE FROM company_code_counter` before re-keying — old values are
  meaningless under the new PK and block `SET NOT NULL` — then the counter is
  re-seeded from the backfilled MOUs so the next allocation can't reuse a
  number.

**Lesson:** "migration generated and hand-checked" has to include running it
against a *populated* database, not just `drizzle-kit generate` plus CI on an
empty one.
